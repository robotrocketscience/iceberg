# R-roundtrip-closure-departure-stage — does the H₂/O₂ departure stage actually help the round-trip, or does its mass eat the gains?

**Status:** SCOPE pre-registered 2026-07-22, before `run.py`. `scope_bounds.py` computes the reactor-mass and single-leg cascade brackets.
**Worker:** worktree-115637 session. Project-owner-directed (chose "round-trip closure, system-level"). The honest system-level test of the departure-architecture thread.
**Predecessors:** `R-electrolysis-departure-stage` (`49f4bb1`) + `R-cryo-hydrogen-storage-saturn` (`3b7dd67`) characterized the H₂/O₂ departure stage *in isolation* (fixed 100 t final mass): it softens the departure reactor from ~1248 kWe (electric spiral) to ~350 kWe and halves departure propellant. This round tests whether that helps the **round-trip mass cascade** once the stage's mass, the reactor's mass, and the propellant are all carried and accelerated.

## The assumption under test

That the departure-architecture comparison done at fixed final mass carries over to the round-trip. The competing effects: H₂/O₂ uses less departure propellant (690 vs 1531 t) *and* a smaller reactor (~350 vs ~1248 kWe), but adds ~37 t of stage hardware. All-electric adds no stage but pays the megawatt-spiral propellant *and* carries a megawatt reactor whose **mass** (at flown specific power) must itself be accelerated through the departure rocket equation. The round-trip metric is the **harvest ratio** — tonnes of water harvested at Saturn per tonne delivered to Earth — and whether delivery is positive.

## Boundary

- **IN:** a backward mass cascade for a target delivered chunk D: reactor mass = P_reactor / specific_power (specific power ∈ {2.4 flown-KRUSTY, 10 mid, 40 aspirational} W/kg; P_reactor = 350 kWe H₂/O₂-electrolysis vs 1248 kWe electric-spiral); departure propellant to accelerate (D + vehicle dry + reactor + propellant tanks) through Δv_dep (8.5 km/s H₂/O₂ impulsive vs 21.9 km/s electric spiral, at Isp 420 vs 800); Earth-capture propellant (H₂/O₂ impulsive 7.3 km/s on the released chunk); harvest ratio and delivered-payload feasibility; the specific-power sensitivity that gates closure.
- **OUT:** the outbound leg and Saturn capture (Earth-launched propellant; not harvested — doesn't affect the harvest ratio); the inbound cruise modeled as a Hohmann coast (no cruise propellant); the harvest-mechanism throughput / bag-mass (locked finding 1 / bag axes own it); reopening Earth-arrival mode (axis 11 falsified — Earth capture is treated as a fixed impulsive H₂/O₂ burn on the chunk, the honest cheapest option); the reactor-lifetime and radiator-mass constraints (axis 20 / titan-4 own them — this round isolates the departure-architecture cascade).

## Pre-registered hypotheses (numeric, falsifiable, before run.py)

**H-H1 [S] — H₂/O₂ gives a much lower (better) harvest ratio than all-electric.** At flown specific power (2.4 W/kg), the H₂/O₂ round-trip harvest ratio is **≤ 50:1** while all-electric is **> 150:1** — a ≥ 3× system improvement — because H₂/O₂ wins on both departure propellant and reactor mass. **Falsified** if H₂/O₂ harvest ratio ≥ all-electric, or if the improvement is < 2×.

**H-H2 [S] — reactor mass is the dominant dead mass at flown specific power.** At 2.4 W/kg the reactor is **≥ 100 t** (H₂/O₂, 350 kWe) and **≥ 400 t** (electric, 1248 kWe) — each dwarfing a 30 t delivered chunk and dominating the departure propellant. **Falsified** if either reactor mass < 50 t at 2.4 W/kg.

**H-H3 [S] — the round-trip "closes" (positive delivery) with H₂/O₂ at flown specific power but only at a very poor harvest ratio (~30–60:1, single-digit-% efficiency); all-electric is effectively non-closing (> 150:1).** Both improve dramatically at aspirational specific power (40 W/kg → H₂/O₂ single-digit:1). **Falsified** if H₂/O₂ delivers ≤ 0 at 2.4 W/kg (doesn't close at all) or if all-electric harvest ratio < 100:1 at 2.4 W/kg (electric closes fine).

**H-H4 [W] — the departure lever materially helps the round-trip but does NOT remove bet #3; the reactor specific-power (mass) wall remains binding.** No configuration closes at a viable harvest ratio (< ~10:1) at flown specific power; viability rides on aspirational specific power (≥ 40 W/kg). **Falsified** if the H₂/O₂ round-trip closes at < 10:1 harvest ratio at flown 2.4 W/kg (departure lever alone rescues closure).

**Honest expected reading (pre-registered):** H-H1/H2/H3/H4 hold. The H₂/O₂ departure stage **materially improves round-trip closure** (a several-fold lower harvest ratio, and the difference between an effectively non-closing all-electric round-trip and a closing-but-inefficient one) — the isolated-analysis win *does* carry over. **But the round-trip remains gated by bet #3's specific-power (reactor-mass) half:** at flown KRUSTY specific power (2.4 W/kg) the few-hundred-kWe reactor is ~100–150 t of dead mass, forcing a ~30–60:1 harvest ratio (single-digit-% delivery efficiency); viable harvest ratios need aspirational specific power (40 W/kg, TRL-2 per power finding 1). So the departure mitigation addresses bet #3's *power* half (peak kWe) but not its *mass* half (specific power) — the two are distinct walls, consistent with titan-4's mass-floor kill and locked finding 5. The system-level verdict: the H₂/O₂ stage is worth adopting (it's the difference between non-closing and inefficient-closing) but it does not by itself make the round-trip viable.

## Method

Deterministic. `run.py`: for D ∈ {20, 30, 50} t × specific power {2.4, 10, 40} W/kg × architecture {H₂/O₂, all-electric}: reactor mass = P/sp; departure burnout mass = D + m_dry + reactor + water-tank + (H₂/O₂ departure tanks); departure propellant via rocket equation at Δv_dep/Isp; Earth-capture propellant = D·(exp(7.3 km/s / v_e,chem)−1); harvest = departure + Earth-capture propellant; harvest ratio = harvest/D; flag closure (finite, positive). Sensitivity table + the specific-power gate. **Anchors:** μ-free (rocket-equation cascade); Δv_dep 8.5 (H₂/O₂ impulsive, Round E) / 21.9 (electric spiral, Round C) km/s; Isp 420 (H₂/O₂) / 800 (electric) s; Earth-capture 7.3 km/s (axis 04); P_reactor 350 (H₂/O₂ electrolysis, Round F/G) / 1248 (electric, Round C) kWe; m_dry 15 t + electrolysis plant 2.2 t (H₂/O₂) + engine 0.5 t (H₂/O₂); water/H₂O₂ tank fractions 3 %/5 %.
